About

Experienced SAP consultant

Who I Am

I am Antti Mokkila, an independent SAP consultant specializing in SAP S/4HANA solutions, with a strong focus on service-related processes. My approach is practical and business-oriented. I am based out of Helsinki, Finland and I work with local and global clients.

Experience

I have over 12 years of hands-on SAP consulting experience from a leading global technology consulting firm, followed by independent consulting engagements. My experience spans multiple SAP S/4HANA projects (5 so far and counting), with roles covering functional lead and solution architect responsibilities.

I have broad experience across multiple SAP S/4HANA modules including Service, Sales (SD), Sourcing and Procurement (MM), Project Systems (PS) and many more. My core expertise lies in SAP S/4HANA Service implementations, including transitions from SAP ECC Customer Service (CS).

Focus Areas

  • SAP S/4HANA Service and Sales implementations
  • Request-to-Service and Order-to-Cash processes
  • Business process design and system integrations
  • End-to-end project delivery
